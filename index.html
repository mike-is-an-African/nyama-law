Family Law, Differently

We litigate.
But we prefer to resolve.

"Collaborative family law is the dignity of resolving things between adults — not the indignity of being torn apart in a courtroom."

The breakdown of a relationship is among the most difficult experiences anyone can face. We bring a different philosophy to it: a commitment to resolve matters constructively, by negotiation, with the threat of court proceedings only where necessary.

We are litigators — fully capable of representing you fiercely in court. But our default posture is collaborative.

01
Resolution through dignified, structured negotiation — not adversarial posturing.
02
Best interests of children placed above the preferences of either parent.
03
Transparent process, clear advice, no surprises in court documents.
04
Litigation remains available — and effective — when collaboration is refused.
