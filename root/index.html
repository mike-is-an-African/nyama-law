About the Firm

Counsel with
a conscience

Nyama Law Chambers was established in 2016 at 4 Bates Street, Milton Park, Harare. We are a full-service firm of attorneys, conveyancers, notaries, commercial lawyers, labour consultants, trademark and patent agents, commissioners of oaths, and child rights consultants.

We bridge the gap in Zimbabwe's justice system, ensuring vulnerable groups receive equal protection under the law.
Specialized expertise in child rights, elder law, and the protection of women, widows and persons with disabilities.
Professional rigour combined with personal care — because behind every case is a life that matters.
