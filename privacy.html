Privacy Notice

How Nyama Law Chambers collects, uses and protects your personal information.

Last updated: January 2026

1. Who We Are

Nyama Law Chambers ("we", "us", "our") is the data controller for personal information you provide to us. We are committed to handling your information responsibly, lawfully and in accordance with the Data Protection Act [Chapter 11:24] of Zimbabwe and applicable international standards.

2. Information We Collect

Depending on the matter, we may collect:

  • Identification information (name, ID number, address, contact details)
  • Financial information necessary for the matter (bank details, asset disclosure)
  • Information relating to the matter itself (family circumstances, employment, property details)
  • Information you submit via this website's enquiry form

3. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Provide the legal services you have instructed us on
  • Verify your identity (anti-money-laundering and KYC checks)
  • Communicate with you about your matter
  • Comply with our legal and regulatory obligations
  • Where you consent, send occasional firm updates or fact sheets

4. Sharing of Information

We do not sell your information. We share it only with parties necessary to advance your matter — for example, the other party's lawyer, the Deeds Registry, banks for bond registration, or courts where proceedings are filed.

5. Retention

We retain client files for the period required by Zimbabwean law and our professional regulator — typically a minimum of seven years from the conclusion of the matter.

6. Your Rights

You have the right to access the personal information we hold about you, request corrections, and (in some circumstances) request deletion. To exercise these rights, contact nyamalawchambers@gmail.com.

7. Website Cookies

This website uses minimal cookies, restricted to functional purposes. We do not use third-party tracking cookies.
