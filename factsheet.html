Significant cases
and legal commentary

Notable Zimbabwean decisions our practitioners follow — judgments we believe have meaningful implications for property, family and constitutional matters.

2015Constitutional Court
Constitutional Law

Mudzuru & Anor v Minister of Justice

Mudzuru & Anor v Minister of Justice, Legal & Parliamentary Affairs & Ors CCZ 12/15

The landmark Constitutional Court ruling that declared child marriage unconstitutional in Zimbabwe. The Court set 18 as the minimum age for any form of marriage — civil, customary or religious — regardless of parental consent.

Outcome / SignificanceSection 22(1) of the Marriage Act and Section 78 of the Customary Marriages Act were struck down. The minimum marriage age was set at 18.
2020Supreme Court
Family Law

Mhondiwa v Mhondiwa

Mhondiwa v Mhondiwa SC 32/20

A significant matrimonial property case clarifying how the indirect contributions of a non-earning spouse — including homemaking and child-rearing — are valued in the division of matrimonial assets on divorce.

Outcome / SignificanceThe Court affirmed that indirect contributions are valued equally to financial contributions in equitable distribution under the Matrimonial Causes Act.
2022High Court
Estate Law

Estate Late Chidziva

Estate Late Chidziva v Chidziva & Ors HH 142-22

A widow inheritance dispute that reinforced the protections offered to surviving spouses under the Deceased Estates Succession Act, particularly where customary norms and statutory law come into tension.

Outcome / SignificanceThe surviving spouse's rights under the Act were upheld over competing customary claims by extended family.
